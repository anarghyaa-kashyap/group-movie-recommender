# NFR-04 / TC-USE-01 — Usability Test Results

Task given to each participant (no help): register → create or join a group → vote on 10 movies → view recommendations → mark watched → rate.

Method: remote test over a video call. Participants opened the app through a shared link (app running in a GitHub Codespace) and the tester observed without helping.

## Participant results

| # | Participant (first name) | Completed unaided? (Y/N) | Time (min) | Where they got stuck |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Akshata | Y | ~5 | None observed |
| 2 | Ahana | Y | ~4 | None observed |
| 3 | Ani | Y | ~4 | None observed |
| 4 | Steve | Y | ~5 | None observed |

Result: 4 / 4 participants completed the full workflow unaided (average about 4.5 minutes, range 4–5).

## Workflow steps completed (all 4 participants)
- Register and log in: completed
- Create or join a group (join code): completed
- Vote Interested / Not Interested on 10 movies: completed
- View the ranked top-10 recommendations: completed
- Mark the movie the group watched: completed
- Rate the movie 1–5 and see the group average: completed

## Outcomes seen during testing
- Test group "null pointers" (members Akshata, Ahana, Ani, Steve) completed 3 movie nights; all three sessions reached the "watched" state.
- Session with 2 raters: recommendations ranked Oldeuboi (2003) first and Intouchables (2011) second, group match about 73%; the group watched Requiem for a Dream and gave a group average of 4.5 from 2 ratings.
- Session with 4 raters: Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse ranked first (group match about 63%); the group watched The Dark Knight and gave a group average of 3.75 from 4 ratings.
- Each recommendation showed title, description, public rating and group match, as required by FR-11.
